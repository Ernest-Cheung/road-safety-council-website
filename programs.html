Programs

Practical skills that save lives.

Our programs meet people where they are, in classrooms, community centers and on the street, with practical skills that save lives.

Safe Kids on the Move

Interactive lessons on crossing safely, seatbelts, bike safety and being seen, with helmet fittings and take-home materials for parents.

Ages 5 to 12 · In-school workshops

Drive Smart Teens

Tackling the biggest risks for young drivers: speeding, phone distraction, peer pressure and impaired driving, through real stories and a safe-driving pledge.

Ages 15 to 19 · Assemblies and small groups

Helmet and Visibility Drive

Free helmets, reflective vests and lights, with fittings by trained volunteers, for cyclists, motorcyclists and children who need them.

All ages · Community events

Safe Streets Advocacy

We work with local government and planners to fix dangerous intersections, calm traffic and make school zones safer.

Community audits · Public forums

Senior Road Safety

Workshops that help older adults stay safe and confident, whether driving, walking or using public transport.

Older adults · Community centers
