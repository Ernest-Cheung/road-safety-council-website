Every journey should end safely.

We work with communities, schools and partners to prevent crashes, save lives and make roads safer for everyone.

Our mission

To reduce deaths and serious injuries on our roads through education, advocacy and community action.

What we do

Education

Hands-on road safety programs for children, teens and new drivers that build safe habits for life.

Advocacy

We work with local government to support safer streets, smarter design and stronger laws.

Community

From helmet giveaways to safety fairs, we bring road safety to the neighborhoods that need it most.

See our programs

Why it matters

Road crashes are a leading cause of death for young people worldwide, and most are preventable. Speeding, distraction, impairment and unsafe street design are problems we can solve together.

Every life saved means a family kept whole.
