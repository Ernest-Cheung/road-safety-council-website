About us

Volunteers, educators and partners united by one belief.

Who we are

The Road Safety Council is a non-profit organization founded in 2022. Registered in California, we serve communities across the United States.

We are volunteers, educators and partners united by one belief: no one should lose their life on the road.

Our vision

A community where every person, whether walking, cycling, riding or driving, can travel safely.

Our values

Prevention first

Most crashes are preventable, so we focus on stopping them before they happen.

Everyone counts

Road safety is for all road users, especially the most vulnerable.

Community-led

We listen to and work alongside the schools, families and neighborhoods we serve.

Transparency

The Road Safety Council is a registered charity in California (registration number CT0287434). For questions about our work or finances, email info@roadsafetycouncil.org.
