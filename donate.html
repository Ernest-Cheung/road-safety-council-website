Donate

Every gift helps someone get home safely.

Your gift funds safety education, protective gear for children and campaigns that change behavior on our roads.

Every gift makes a difference. A small donation can provide a helmet and fitting for a child, reflective gear for a family, or a road safety workshop for a whole classroom.

Protective gear

Helmets, reflective vests and bike lights for children and families who need them.

Classroom workshops

Road safety lessons for schools, from first crossings to first driving lessons.

Safer streets

Community audits and campaigns that push for safer intersections and school zones.
